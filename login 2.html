About MindMate

MindMate is an AI-driven mental health companion designed to detect early signs of mental stress, anxiety, and depression through intelligent conversation and real-time facial emotion analysis. Developed as part of the AI & AR-Based Doctor Application initiative at the Indian Institute of Information Technology, Design and Manufacturing (IIITDM) Kurnool, this project combines Flask-based AI services, OpenCV camera modules, and Natural Language Processing (NLP) models to assess and monitor a user’s emotional well-being.
